Conveyor Safety Instructions

Do’s ✅

  • Ensure all workers are trained in conveyor operation and safety procedures.
  • Inspect belts, rollers, and motors for wear or damage. Check alignment and tension regularly.
  • Always keep guards in place to prevent accidental contact with moving parts.
  • Maintain a clutter-free area around the conveyor. Clean up spilled materials immediately.
  • Always disconnect power before performing any maintenance or repair.
  • Wear close-fitting clothing and appropriate personal protective equipment.
  • Keep loads within conveyor weight limits. Distribute items evenly to avoid strain.
  • Use signals or intercoms for communication. Display warning signs.
  • Investigate strange sounds immediately.

Don’ts ❌

  • Never stand, sit, or ride on a conveyor belt.
  • Never disable emergency stops, guards, or other safety features.
  • Avoid placing excessive weight.
  • Never clear jams or adjust while conveyor is running.
  • Do not wear loose clothing or jewelry — these can get caught and cause injuries.
  • Do not leave loose tools on the conveyor.
  • Only trained personnel should operate.
